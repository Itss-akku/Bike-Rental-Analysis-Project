# Bike Rental Analysis Project:

## Problem Statement:-
Analyzing bike-sharing data helps understand how weather, time, and holidays affect bike rentals. This project uses Excel skills to clean, combine, and study datasets to fi nd useful patterns that improve business planning.
ganized data leads to better decisions faster.

## Question Answered in the Project:-
- What is Average Rentals/Hours
- Which hours has higher Rentals(Peak Hours)
- Is there any change in Bike Rental Demand on weekday/weekend or holiday
- How Weather affect Bike Rentals Like Temp, Humidity, Weather Situations
- What are the contribution of Casual vs registered customers, who is the most contributor to bike rentals casual or registered.
- How many bike rented throughtout the data period 
- Which month has higher bike rentals.

## Process used:-
-  Data Import from the dataset into the main Bike rental analysis file
-  Preprocess Dataset1,2 and 3.
-  Merge Dataset1 into Dataset2 using Vlookup in excel sheets & in Power query using Merge Option
-  Consolidate Dataset3 with Updated Dataset derived from merging Dataset1 & Dataset2 using Hlookup or simple paste option & in power query using append feature.
-  Load the final Dataset-A into the Datamodel for final Data analysis some pivot table is created to analyse the data a support sheet is created in the xlsb file for the same.
-  Charts has been Created thru the pivot tables & some slices has been added to create and interactive dashboard for better analysis
-  Interpret the findings -  

## Key Insights
- Demand Increases in Morning & Evening(Peak Hours are morning 8 to 10 AM & Evening 4 to 7PM
- Rentals are higher on Working Days as Registered customers are higher in comparison of casual customers & major customers are expected to working persons who book bikes on Morning & evening hours.
- Casual Bike Rentals Demand increases on Weekends (i.e. Sat & Sun & Registered customers Bike Rental Demand decreases on Weekend.
- We' analyse Demands are higher in Bright sunny weather & customers rarely rent bikes in High thunderstrom weather.
- Average Hourly Rental booking is 55 per hour & 8% of Customers are Casual customers and major 92% contribution in Demand is from registered customers.
